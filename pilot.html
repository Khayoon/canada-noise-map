Integration

One coordinate lookup. Both periods.

  1. Tell us your use case, required cities and expected monthly volume.
  2. Receive a pilot key for your server. Geocode addresses separately using your chosen provider.
  3. Call /v1/noise for a point or /v1/noise/batch for up to 100 locations. Handle missing data explicitly.
  4. Display the model date, limitations and OpenStreetMap attribution alongside the result.

What the estimate can tell you
These are modelled outdoor averages: LAeq,16h by day and LAeq,8h by night. Individual building shielding, terrain and barriers are approximated rather than traced. Rail frequency, aircraft operations and typical site activity include assumptions. Events such as construction or sirens are not predicted.
There is no independently established error range for an individual address. Historical Toronto population-exposure comparisons are partly fitted and describe distributions, not measured property accuracy. Missing estimates should never be labelled “quiet”.
The quiet score is a 50–100 display scale, not a percentile or health rating. The hosted API serves a combined noise raster; it does not currently identify each source's contribution at a point.
