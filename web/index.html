    About this map

    This is a free, open transportation noise map for Canadian cities, in the spirit of the noise layer on US real-estate sites and the US DOT's National Transportation Noise Map — which Canada does not have. Every road, railway and runway in OpenStreetMap is treated as a line source with a class-based emission level; sound spreads with distance and ground/air absorption and all sources are summed in the energy domain. The result is an estimated daytime A-weighted equivalent level (LAeq) on a ~15 m grid.

    Limitations — please read

    • It is a model, not a measurement. Traffic volumes are inferred from road class, lanes and speed limits, not counted.
    • Buildings, terrain and noise barriers are ignored, so levels behind a row of houses or a highway berm are overestimated.
    • Airports are modelled as line sources along runways: real flight paths, runway usage and night restrictions are not.
    • Coverage stops at the edge of each city extract; roads just outside it are missing, so edges read quieter than they are.
    • Local sources (construction, bars, industry, sirens) are not included.
