    About this map

    This public demo estimates outdoor environmental noise in Canadian cities. Mapped transport and site sources contribute sound energy, which is propagated and summed in a precomputed model. The result is an estimated A-weighted equivalent level (LAeq) on a roughly 15 m grid, for two periods: daytime (07:00–23:00, LAeq,16h) and nighttime (23:00–07:00, LAeq,8h).

    Road emission uses a simplified, overall A-weighted formulation based on CNOSSOS-EU. Traffic flow, speed and heavy-vehicle share affect the level. Propagation is an approximation; this map is not a full implementation of the CNOSSOS-EU assessment method.

    Compare with Toronto's measured and modelled noise study or Bruitparif's monitoring stations. A monitoring station measures its own location; continuous map colours are estimates between locations.

    How accurate is it?

    Do not interpret a precise map value as a measured reading, an indoor level or a guarantee for a property. Short peaks and individual events are outside these period averages.

    How to read it

    The quiet score is a display scale from 50 to 100: 100 at 40 dB or less, 50 at 80 dB or more. It is not a percentile or a health rating. ≤ and ≥ mark display-range limits.

    Limitations — please read

    • It is a model, not a measurement. Traffic-count coverage, age and matching quality vary. Regional input summaries do not establish confidence at an individual address.
    • Buildings, terrain and noise barriers are not modelled individually — shielding is approximated by an average attenuation, so levels behind a row of houses or a highway berm are overestimated.
    • Coverage stops at the edge of each city extract; roads just outside it are missing, so edges read quieter than they are.
    • Rail frequencies and site activity are assumptions. Weather and changes since the source data were collected are not represented.
    • Night is modelled. Road traffic is split into assumed day and night shares; rail, aircraft and site operations use modelled schedules. This is not a live traffic or event feed.
